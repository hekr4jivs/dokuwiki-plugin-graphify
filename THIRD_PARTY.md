# Third-party dependency

`vendor/vis-network.min.js` is the unmodified standalone UMD build of vis-network
9.1.9. It is used locally; browsers do not fetch it from a CDN.

Source: https://unpkg.com/vis-network@9.1.9/standalone/umd/vis-network.min.js
